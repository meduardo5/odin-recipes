Agua de Jamaica

How to prepare homemade agua de Jamiaca.

Agua de jamaica is a classic Mexican hibiscus agua fresca. It's floral, refreshing, and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dried hibiscus flowers (flor de jamaica)
  • 6 cups water
  • 1/2-3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • Ice, for serving

Steps

  1. Rinse the hibiscus. Rinse the dried flowers under cool water to remove any dust or debris.
  2. Make the infusion. Bring 4 cups of water to a boil. Add the hibiscus flowers, reduce the heat, and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
  3. Strain. Remove from the heat and strain the deep-red liquid into a large pitcher. Press the flowers gently with a spoon to extract their flavor.
  4. Sweeten. Stir in the sugar while the liquid is still warm so it dissolves easily.
  5. Dilute. Add the remaining 2 cups of cold water. Taste and add more sugar if needed.
  6. Chill and serve. Refrigerate until cold, then serve over plenty of ice.
